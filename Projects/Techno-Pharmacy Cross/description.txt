Techno-Pharmacy Cross
---
Techno-Pharmacy Cross is a stylized 3D motion piece inspired by the “techno pharmacy signs” trend — hypnotic LED crosses synced to drum & bass / rave energy. I rebuilt the concept in my own visual language, focusing on tight rhythm, graphic readability, and punchy light choreography.
